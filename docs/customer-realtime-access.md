# Delayed research and paid Telegram

Free confirmed accounts see eligible prospective sports plays and forward market trades after a minimum 30-minute delay. Immutable first-observed versions independently delay entries, results, strategy matches and trade exits. Research withdrawals remove rejected/quarantined or absent records; missing or stale sources do not replace the prior snapshot. Every current eligible record is captured (source cap 10,000); the customer page displays the latest 100. Publication follows the existing independent 15-minute business sync, so release occurs at least 30 minutes after first observation, generally 30–45 minutes plus collection latency. Initial publication has a 30-minute warmup. Play visibility and alert delivery eligibility remain separate decisions.

Server-owned entitlements require live Stripe Checkout completion, captured payment, matching account/customer, no refund/dispute, and current paid subscription service period. The frontend refreshes provider evidence before backend real-time reads. Unverified, expired, refunded, test-mode and browser metadata grants fail closed to delayed access. Public paid checkout remains disabled until PAID_CHECKOUT_ENABLED is explicitly configured with a reviewed live price and trusted origin.

Telegram linking is private, single-use and one-to-one. Private-channel invites are short-lived join requests bound to the Telegram identity and verified payment. Admission rechecks Stripe. Reconciliation removes mapped members after expiry, cancellation or refund; provider outages do not cause unsupported removals. Existing unmapped channel members and old links require a separate membership audit: Telegram cannot enumerate all subscribers. This patch cannot prove those historical members paid.

Owner QA permits one labelled $1 live charge, no renewal, with access fixed to 30 minutes after capture. QA is distinct from ordinary customers/revenue and natural betting proof. Labelled verification notifications go to the user-designated Telegram Enterprise chat. A prior test sent to the wrong destination was removed; the original and corrected receipts remain in the audit trail. Refund after verification and verify revocation. No stored card is used; the user enters a real card on Stripe Checkout. An authorization or synthetic receipt does not complete this test.

Backend hooks need CUSTOMER_BILLING_ORIGIN set to the new frontend. Branch preview needs MEMBER_FEED_BACKEND_URL plus existing service/source configuration. QA_CHECKOUT_USER_ID/ENABLED/RUN_ID/ORIGIN and ENTERPRISE_CHAT_ID are server-only, branch-scoped. Never publish credentials or private invites in evidence.

## October 8 actual verification

One $1 live Payment Link Checkout completed and captured; the account-bound provider verifier applied and refreshed its QA grant. The access policy accepted that account and rejected a foreign account and ordinary-customer QA use. Telegram returned a delivery receipt. The full $1 refund succeeded and fresh provider verification revoked the grant. The original message used the wrong destination; it was deleted and the confirmation was sent to the user-designated Enterprise chat with a separate receipt. Product, price and one-payment link were deactivated.

This proves live capture, server grant, access-policy checks, Telegram transport and refund/revocation. It does not prove browser admission to the paid channel or a deployed customer receiving a natural betting alert. The initial previews are READY; later Enterprise-routing and manual-publication safeguards remain in the draft source until another preview is deployed.
